🍭 {{ t('dr') }} Guidelines

Diabetic Retinopathy occurs when high blood sugar damage microvascular vessels in the retina, causing leaking or proliferative bleeding.

  • Glycemic Indexing: Monitor HbA1c levels regularly, maintaining a clinical target of under 7.0%.
  • Vascular Nutrition: Eat high-fiber whole foods (barleys, lentils, berries, almonds) that prevent insulin spikes.
  • Avoid Ocular Strain: Avoid severe weightlifting or inverted postures that might burst fragile new blood vessels (neovascularization).

👁️ {{ t('glaucoma') }} Guidelines

Often referred to as the 'silent thief of sight', Glaucoma gradual damages the optic nerve due to blockages in fluid drainage and high pressure.

  • Fluid Management: Limit caffeine intake and drink water in small quantities throughout the day to prevent sudden pressure spikes.
  • Sleep Safety: Use a raised pillow to elevate the head. Never sleep with face down or arms resting against your closed eyelids.
  • Antioxidant Diet: Focus on dietary nitrates (spinach, kale, lettuce) which enhance nitric oxide production and visual microcirculation.

🧬 {{ t('amd') }} Guidelines

Age-Related Macular Degeneration breaks down macular tissues responsible for sharp, straight-ahead reading and facial recognition.

  • Lutein Shielding: Lutein and Zeaxanthin act as internal sunglasses. Consume them in spinach, eggs, and yellow peppers.
  • Omega-3 Fatty Acids: Retina membranes contain high concentrations of DHA. Eat salmon, mackerel, and walnuts.
  • UV Blocking: Wear dark polarized sunglasses labeled UV400 when outdoors to reduce oxidative light stress on the macula.

💻 Digital Eye Strain Prevention

With increased screen time, digital eye strain (asthenopia) leads to dry eyes, headaches, and blurred vision.

  • 20-20-20 Rule: Every 20 minutes of screen work, take a 20-second break and look at something 20 feet away.
  • Lubrication: Blink deliberately and use preservative-free artificial tears if working in air-conditioned environments.
  • Basking Light: Ensure your workspace is well-lit and screen brightness matches room lighting to reduce pupil glare.
